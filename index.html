Number Lab

A claim that says "for every" only needs one allowed case where it fails to be shown false. That one case is a counterexample. Test values one at a time. You can type integers, decimals, and simple fractions such as 3/4 or -5/2.

Rectangle Lab: Area Versus Perimeter

Build pairs of rectangles and check them against the claim. A pair only tests the claim when the claim condition is met. A pair that meets the condition but breaks the conclusion is a counterexample.

Lengths and widths are in the same unit. Areas are in square units.

Reverse Mode: Repair a Broken Claim

This claim has already been broken. Your job is to fix it: restrict the domain, add a condition, or weaken the conclusion, so the repaired claim actually holds. Pick a repair and the app will test it on a sweep of values, including the value or pair that broke the original.
